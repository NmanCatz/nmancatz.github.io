About Me

I am a PC gamer and a longtime fan of GTA V. I have been creating virtual photography in Los Santos since 2019, and I am still having fun exploring new ideas and improving my style.

I love mixing my real-life love of cats into my photos to create a unique aesthetic. I mainly photograph my avatar, but I have also started creating reels that I hope capture emotion, nostalgia, and atmosphere. I am always down to collaborate with fellow creators!

I always treat people with the most respect and kindness possible. I am currently studying Visual Communications at NAIT with the goal of becoming a graphic designer. I have also started diving into web development and coded both this site and my crew website using Visual Studio Code.
